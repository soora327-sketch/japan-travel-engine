Japan Luggage & Hands-Free Travel

We are researching practical luggage forwarding and storage options across Japan for travelers who want to move around without carrying bags all day.

  • Luggage storage and temporary bag drop options
  • Airport → hotel and hotel → airport luggage movement
  • Hotel → hotel forwarding and same-day / next-day constraints
  • Coin locker alternatives for larger bags
  • Hands-free travel planning around Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, and major stations

What this is not

This is not yet a complete booking service, and we do not claim to operate luggage forwarding ourselves. The goal is to compare practical options and build useful traveler-facing guides before recommending any service.

Planned demand test

The next test will measure whether travelers need a simple Japan luggage finder before investing in a larger website or database.

Pilot concept

Tokyo Restaurant Booking Help

This service is being prepared as a small manual pilot under Japan Travel Engine. It is for international visitors who already picked one Tokyo restaurant and mainly need help because Japanese phone booking is the barrier.

Current MVP scope

  • Tokyo restaurants only
  • One customer-selected restaurant
  • 1–4 guests
  • Preferably at least 72 hours before the visit
  • A real human Japanese speaker calls the restaurant and sends the result in English
  • Reservation success is not guaranteed

Not included in the pilot

  • Hotels, transport, flights, tours, tickets, or itinerary planning
  • Restaurant discovery or concierge recommendations
  • Restaurant bill payment, deposits, or card guarantees paid by us
  • Complex allergy, medical, or safety handling
  • Large groups or special event arrangements
About

Who this is for

Japan Travel Engine is for international visitors who want practical answers before and during a Japan trip.

  • First-time or repeat travelers planning logistics in English
  • Travelers with luggage, station, airport, or hotel-transfer questions
  • Travelers who already know what they want but face a Japan-specific process barrier
Editorial approach

Practical before broad.

We are not trying to publish a giant travel blog. We focus on narrow, high-friction travel problems, test whether the information is useful, and expand only when there is real demand.
